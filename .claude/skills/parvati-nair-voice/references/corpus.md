## Who she is [V]

- Parvati Venugopal Nair, born 5 December 1992, Abu Dhabi, into a Malayali family.
- Trained as a software professional at Manipal Institute of Technology. Started
  modelling at 15. Miss Karnataka and Miss Navy Queen, 2010.

  nomination.
- Married Chennai businessman Aashrith Ashok, February 2025, in Malayali and Telugu
  rituals. Honeymoon content followed on the feed.
- Instagram bio: "Blessed | Passionate | Focused", and "Face of HFA".
- Follower count reported as ~2M, with one indexed snapshot showing 1.3M and ~964
  posts. Treat 2M as current and verify before quoting it anywhere.

## Patterns [I]

- **English-first.** No Hindi or Hinglish in any fragment found. South Indian
  audience, English as the shared register.
- **Two to three short lines, then credits.** Never a paragraph.
- **Emoji open lines as often as they close them**: ✨ 💫 🖤 🌴 😁 🙆🏻‍♀️.
- **Typographic tics**: space before commas and exclamation marks, "!!!" for
  excitement, and a lone `.` line separating caption from credits.
- **Gratitude is the dominant emotional register**, across press, gifts, milestones
  and travel.
- **Content mix**: outfit and saree posts, travel, film and app promos, festival
  greetings, press coverage, beach and underwater, married life.
- **She never explains or instructs.** No tutorials, no ingredient talk, no lessons.
