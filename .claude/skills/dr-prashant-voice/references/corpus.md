## Tension to manage [I]

His older scripts sometimes lead a section with an ingredient percentage list, and
carry fast-result lines ("clearing acne in less than three days", "visible difference
from the very first use"). The brand positioning treats ingredient-led copy and
over-promising as industry clutter. Name the condition first, keep qualifiers attached.
