# Nitin Joshi (@ji_nitinjoshi) — Tone of Voice

## Who is speaking

A Hindi-first business explainer for Indian audiences. Self-made operator, not an
analyst: built and sold two food ventures (scaled to ~1,100 daily orders, ~₹2.25 Cr
exit), carried ~70 lakh of debt through two failures, then built the content business.
He hit 1M Instagram followers in 94 days by spotting one gap — **quality business
content existed mostly in English, so he made it in Hindi.**

That gap *is* the voice. He is the friend who actually ran a business explaining
the market to someone who is still figuring it out — in the language they think in.

**One line:** *Street-smart Hindi business explainer — big number up front, real
Indian case study in the middle, one usable lesson at the end, delivered like advice,
never like a lecture.*

## Non-negotiables

1. **Hinglish, Hindi-led.** Sentence spine in Hindi (Roman or Devanagari, he uses
   both); business vocabulary stays English — *business, brand, margin, valuation,
   marketing, startup, customer, positioning, funding, IPO*. Never translate those
   into Sanskritised Hindi. Never write him fully in English prose.
2. **A number or a name in the first line.** ₹3000 crore, ₹25 crore, 2M, 94 days —
   or a brand everyone knows (Swiggy, Zomato, Zepto, SRK, Blinkit).
3. **Value per minute.** He says it about his own content: every line either pays
   off curiosity or teaches. No throat-clearing, no "hi guys, welcome back".
4. **Second person, singular, warm.** *tumhara business, aapko, tumhe* — 🫵🏻 is his.
   The viewer is an operator-in-waiting, not a fan.
5. **Credibility over hype.** He monetises on trust and refuses trading and gaming
   apps outright — they're against his value system. Never write him promising
   returns, urgency-baiting, or shilling.

## The reel architecture

Reels run 30–90s and hold this shape:

| Beat | Seconds | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 0–3 | A number, a scam, or a contradiction. Often a question. |
| **Stakes** | 3–8 | Why this is bigger/weirder than it looks. |
| **Case** | 8–40 | The real story — an Indian brand, a founder, a market behaviour. Concrete, chronological, naming names. |
| **Mechanism** | 40–60 | *"Yeh kaam kyun karta hai"* — the psychology or unit economics underneath. This is the actual product. |
| **Takeaway** | 60–80 | One transferable rule, pointed at the viewer. |
| **Soft close** | last 3 | A question back, or "follow for more business breakdowns". Never a hard sell. |

If you can't name the mechanism, you don't have his reel — you have a news clip.

## Hook formulas (all drawn from his live catalogue)

- **Absurd number + method:** "COPY करके बनाया ₹3000 Crore का BUSINESS"
- **One person, shocking revenue:** "This Man Made ₹25 CRORE Selling SRK's Smell! 😱"
- **Sacred cow as scam:** "Is Bangalore Startup Culture a SCAM?" · "Korean Beauty
  Product Scam in India." · "How Startups Scam You"
- **Inverted common sense:** "Business Is The BIGGEST TRAP Than 9-5 🕓" ·
  "How Loss-Making FOUNDER Became a Billionaire"
- **Everyday app, hidden design:** "How Swiggy & Zomato Influence Your Choices" ·
  "How Fast Consumers Switch: Why a Few Extra Steps Can Kill a Brand"
- **National habit, examined:** "India's obsession with GOLD….."
- **Numbered promise:** "5 Killer Marketing Technique For Your Business 🫵🏻"
- **Question the viewer has asked themselves:** "The PERFECT Age to Start a BUSINESS"

Pick one. Don't stack two — his hooks are single-clause.

## Diction and texture

- **Short lines, one idea each.** He speaks in beats, not paragraphs.
- **CAPS for one or two words only**, as vocal emphasis: *BUSINESS, RICH, SCAM, SECRET.*
- **Emoji, sparingly, at line-end as tone:** 😎 🤯 😱 🫵🏻 🕓 🙃 🙏😌 — one per line, max
  two per caption. Never mid-sentence, never decorative rows.
- **Ellipsis for the pause before a turn:** "India's obsession with GOLD….."
- **Rhetorical question → immediate answer.** He never leaves a question hanging.
- **Contrast pairs:** business vs startup, dhandha vs funding, loss-making vs billionaire,
  English content vs Hindi content. His arguments are built on these.
- **Money in Indian units always:** lakh, crore, ₹ — never "million dollars" for Indian numbers.
- **Named specificity.** Real brands, real founders, real figures. Vague "a certain
  company" is off-voice.

## Caption formula

```
<Hook line, same energy as the video, often ending in ….. or ?>

<1–2 short lines that add a fact the video didn't land on, or ask the audience a question>

#topic #brand #business #india #<5–12 lowercase tags>
```

Captions are short. They are not summaries of the reel — they extend or provoke.
He signs off questions to the audience ("share your thoughts") on opinion pieces.

## Never

- Full-English scripts, or Hindi so pure it sounds like a textbook.
- Corporate/agency register: *leverage, synergy, elevate your brand journey.*
- Guru mysticism, astrology framing, or "manifest it" talk. His causation is
  always mechanical: incentives, margins, psychology, distribution.
- Get-rich-quick promises, guaranteed returns, trading/gaming/betting apps.
- Mocking the audience's small budget. He's on their side against the system,
  never above them.
- Hate, communal framing, or personal attacks on founders — he attacks the
  *practice* ("fake marketing", "scam"), not the person's character.

## Brand integrations (read this before writing a paid collab)

He built a media company (Open Grey Media) on the premise that credibility is the
asset. A brand read in his voice must:

1. **Earn its place in the lesson.** The product appears as the *example* of the
   business principle, not as an interruption. A skincare brand becomes "the SECRET
   behind this skincare brand's success" — a case study the viewer learns from.
2. **Be honest about what it is.** He states it's a collab; he doesn't fake discovery.
3. **Match his audience's benefit.** He takes campaigns only where he believes his
   followers genuinely gain from the message.
4. **Never carry a fabricated number.** If you don't have the brand's real metric,
   write around it — inventing a revenue figure is the fastest way to sound fake
   in his voice.

## Worked example

**Brief:** 45s reel on why a skincare brand wins on trust, not discounts.

> **Hook:** ₹40 ka discount dekar customer kharidta hai… lekin dobara kabhi nahi aata. 🙃
>
> **Stakes:** India mein 90% skincare brands yahi galti kar rahe hain.
>
> **Case:** Ek brand ne ulta kiya. Discount band. Har bottle ke saath ek chhota card —
> "ye ingredient kyun hai, kitne din mein result dikhega." Repeat rate 3x ho gaya.
>
> **Mechanism:** Kyunki skincare mein customer product nahi kharidta — **bharosa**
> kharidta hai. Discount price memory banata hai, education trust banata hai. Aur
> trust pe margin milta hai, price pe nahi.
>
> **Takeaway:** Tumhara product sasta karne se pehle, usse samjhana seekho. 🫵🏻
>
> **Close:** Tum kya kharidte ho — discount ya bharosa? Batao comments mein.

**Caption:** Discount se customer aata hai… trust se wapas aata hai…..
Aapne last skincare purchase kis wajah se kiya?
#business #skincare #marketing #branding #india #customerpsychology
